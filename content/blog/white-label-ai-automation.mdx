White label AI automation lets an agency sell a workflow — inbox, documents, CRM, or phone — without hiring an AI team first. The client still calls the agency. The studio builds behind that name. Hushlane Labs does this for agencies in the United States, the United Kingdom, Australia, and Canada, with delivery based in India. The constraint that matters is not the model. It is that a person still approves anything that could embarrass the client.

## White label AI automation is a service, not a demo

Agencies are being asked for “AI” in proposals that were, last year, a CRM cleanup or a faster follow-up. The honest version of that offer is a mapped process, a small set of integrations, a review queue, and a runbook an operator can follow. It is not a chatbot dropped on a homepage to see what happens.

The service page for [white-label AI automation](/services/white-label-ai-automation) lists the work as inbox, document, CRM, and phone workflows. Deliverables are a process map, integrations, a review queue, an evaluation set, and an operator runbook. The process is: collect samples, draw the boundary, connect with the least privilege that works, trial the automation on live work, and leave a log. Money movement, customer messages, and filings stay with a person in the loop until someone has measured the errors.

That last sentence is the whole product decision. An automation that sends a message in the client’s voice can do damage a late website cannot. The boundary is drawn before the connection is made, not after the first bad send.

## What you do not need to hire first

You do not need a research team to sell a bounded automation you understand. You need someone who can name the process, the system of record, and the action a human must still approve. The studio supplies the build: the integration, the queue, and the log. The agency supplies the client relationship and the judgment about what the client was actually promised.

A named lead stays on the work. You should know who is in the repository next month. Reviews happen in a shared window in your business hours, with written notes that do not depend on someone being awake. Australian reviews are booked on purpose. The rest is async. There is no contact with the end client unless you ask in writing.

The NDA comes first. A mutual NDA comes before credentials, customer data, or a document that names the client. Secrets stay in a vault the agency owns. Production credentials are listed, temporary, and removed at handoff. Connecting an automation with a personal login is how a quiet tool becomes a security incident.

Zappio is the AI example on this site. It is a voice AI CRM for Indian real estate teams. The live product is at zappio.in. It covers a WhatsApp Business API integration, a voice AI calling pipeline, and lead management. The problem was ordinary: phone calls, WhatsApp chats, and the lead record were separate, so a conversation could finish without the CRM showing what had just happened.

The result line is specific and limited. Zappio is live in production at zappio.in, automating first response and follow-up for real estate leads through WhatsApp and AI voice calls. The stack named on the page is the WhatsApp Business API, voice AI calling, and the CRM. The role was design, build, and deploy, end to end. This note does not add a pickup rate, a lead count, or a time saved. Those figures are not published as a result on the case study, so they do not belong here. Screenshots on the case study show the product: a dashboard, call agents in Hindi and English, approved WhatsApp templates, and a lead record with qualification status, call history, and the WhatsApp thread.

An agency reading that page should notice what was automated and what was not claimed. First response and follow-up moved into the product. The case study does not say a human left the process. The service page’s review queue exists for that reason. A voice call that qualifies a lead still needs a place where a person can see the outcome and correct it.

## How to sell it without overselling it

Sell the workflow you can draw. “We will add AI” is not a scope. “We will log the WhatsApp thread on the lead and place the first follow-up call, and a person reviews the qualification” is a scope. The second sentence can be built. The first sentence can only be regretted.

Put the refusal in the proposal. A serious studio can name work it will not automate. If the partner agrees to every sentence, they have not read it. Evaluation is part of the delivery, not a slide: a set of real samples, a check against those samples, and a log of what the automation did. Without that, the agency cannot tell a client why a message went out.

Also separate the website from the automation. Acredge, at acredge.ai, includes AI-assisted recommendations and queries inside a property platform. That is a feature of a web product, on Next.js, React, and Google Cloud. Lumic, at lumic.co.in, turns market data and news into AI-driven insights across web and mobile, with Next.js, React, Flutter, and LLM-powered insights. Neither of those sentences is “we bolted a chatbot on.” If the proposal is a marketing site with a widget, use the web page. If the proposal is the workflow itself, use the automation page.

## The operating model after launch

An automation that nobody can operate is a liability with an API key. The runbook is a deliverable because the agency’s team, or the client’s team, has to know what the job does on a Monday. The log is how you answer “what did it send?” The review queue is how a bad action waits for a person.

Access ends when the engagement ends, unless you already chose a retainer before kickoff. That choice is made before the work starts, not on the last Friday. The code belongs to the party named in the statement of work once the related invoices are paid. Repositories transfer at handoff. We do not keep a private fork to resell. The same build can ship under the agency’s brand. Hushlane takes a credit only if you ask for one in writing.

Communication stays in your channel. One lead. A weekly note. Booked reviews. No surprise introduction to the end client. If a date has to move, you hear it before that day, with what changed. You get a reply to a new brief within 24 hours on business days. If we are the wrong studio, that reply says so.

Write down the process in one page: the trigger, the system of record, the action, and the step a person must approve. Attach samples, not passwords. Sign the NDA before the samples include customer records. Then send the brief. The first reply is a fit check.
