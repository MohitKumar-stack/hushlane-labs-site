## Overview

Lumic is an AI-powered financial intelligence app for Indian investors. The live product is at [lumic.co.in](https://lumic.co.in). It covers AI-driven market insights and a consumer-facing web and app experience.

## Problem

Investors were seeing market news without a clear reading of what it meant for them, and the explanation had to work in a product a person would actually open.

## Solution

- AI-driven market insights written for an investor, not a trading desk.
- A consumer-facing web experience for the same product.
- A consumer-facing app experience around that reading.

## Tech stack

- Next.js
- React
- Flutter
- LLM-powered insights

## My role

Designed, built, and deployed end to end.

## Result

Live product at lumic.co.in, built end to end across web and mobile, turning market data and news into clear AI-driven insights for Indian investors.
