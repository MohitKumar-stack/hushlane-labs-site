Lumic stands in for a product where the agency owns the relationship and the reading experience has to feel finished, not like a model demo. The model drafts. A person publishes. That split is the product.

The public site and the app are built so the publisher’s name is the only one a reader needs. Credentials, stores, and the content system sit in the publisher’s accounts.
