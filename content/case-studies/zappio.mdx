Zappio is a placeholder for a voice product an agency might sell to a brokerage. The useful part is not the model. It is the handoff: the call has to land in the system the agents already trust, and a person still confirms a booking.

The agency stays on the account. Store listings, CRM credentials, and the support address belong to them. Hushlane builds the path and the review screen, then leaves the keys in the agency’s vault.
