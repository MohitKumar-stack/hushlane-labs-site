## Overview

Zappio is a voice AI CRM for Indian real estate. The live product is at [zappio.in](https://zappio.in). It covers WhatsApp Business API integration, a voice AI calling pipeline, and lead management for real estate teams.

## Problem

Phone calls, WhatsApp chats, and the lead record were separate. A conversation could finish without the team’s CRM showing what had just happened.

## Solution

- WhatsApp Business API integration, so chats stay with the lead.
- A voice AI calling pipeline for conversations that happen by phone.
- Lead management built for real estate teams.

## Tech stack

- WhatsApp Business API
- Voice AI calling
- CRM

## My role

Designed, built, and deployed end to end.

## Result

Live in production at zappio.in, automating first response and follow-up for real estate leads through WhatsApp and AI voice calls.
