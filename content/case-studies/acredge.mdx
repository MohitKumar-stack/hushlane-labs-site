## Overview

Acredge is a real estate proptech platform. The live site is [acredge.ai](https://acredge.ai).

## Problem

Property buyers and investors deal with scattered listings, unverified information, and slow responses, while developers and agents lose leads to poor follow-up.

## Solution

- Smart property search and discovery with filters.
- AI-assisted recommendations and queries.
- Lead and listing management for agents and developers.

## Tech stack

- Next.js
- React
- Google Cloud

## My role

Designed, built, and deployed end to end.

## Result

Live at acredge.ai, built end to end.
